Sources & Data Registry — registered · producing signal
Sources are inputs, not structure. Every record carries its source as a field and nothing in this terminal is navigated by source — a reader wants to know what changed and what to do, not which site it came from. Sources churn; the signal taxonomy is what stays stable. A registered source is one the pipeline is authorised to draw from, not one currently producing. Confidence reflects source class, not individual accuracy. Check terms of use and prefer official API or open-data routes over scraping wherever offered.

Social Intelligence Medium · manual watch only, never automated
Leadership posts from Vice Chancellors, Provosts, International Directors and Enrolment leads frequently carry partnership and expansion news days before the formal release — likely the highest-value gap in this registry. It is deliberately not wired into the pipeline: LinkedIn offers no open API for this, and scraping personal profiles raises terms-of-service and ethical questions that public-portal and trade-press collection does not. Treat as a curated watch list of 10–15 accounts checked periodically, and as an early-warning trigger to look for the official announcement — never as a standalone confirmed signal.
